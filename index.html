ABOUT OUR DHAMA

Tradition | Healing | Spiritual Wellbeing

Sri Rajarajeshwari Adhyathmika and Ayurveda Dhama is envisioned as a sanctuary for authentic Ayurveda and holistic living, bringing together classical Ayurvedic traditions with spiritual practices.

Our approach includes Ayurvedic consultation, Panchakarma, yoga, meditation, mantra chikitse, spiritual guidance and personalised diet & lifestyle counselling.
